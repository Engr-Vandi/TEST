Our Story

A Legacy of
Refined Hospitality

Since 1987, Aurum Grand Hotel has stood as a beacon of exceptional hospitality. Nestled in the heart of Port Harcourt, our hotel blends classical elegance with modern luxury — offering every guest an experience that transcends the ordinary.

From our meticulously curated rooms to our award-winning restaurant and spa, every detail has been crafted to deliver moments of pure indulgence.
